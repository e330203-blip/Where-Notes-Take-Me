About the journey.

A personal space for learning, experimenting, creating and sharing music.

Hello

I'm learning by doing.

Where Notes Take Me is a personal music project built around my journey of learning the kalimba. Instead of keeping every small discovery in a notebook, I wanted to turn the process into something I could share.

This website is a record of that process: the things that work, the things that don't, the songs I practice, and the melodies I create myself.
